# Stop Everything

Two minutes before a meeting, a meme takes over your Mac screen until you join.

**[Download for Mac](https://github.com/deetrg/stop-everything/releases/latest/download/Stop-Everything-mac-arm64.zip)** · Free · macOS 14+ · Apple silicon · Website: https://stopeverything.pages.dev

## What it does

- Connect Google Calendar, or paste any calendar link (secret iCal address).
- Two minutes before a meeting (30 s to 10 min, your choice), every screen is taken over, above everything, on every display.
- One button joins the call. Routines, tasks and solo calendar blocks are left alone.
- Nine reaction-meme characters (The Office, This Is Fine, SpongeBob, Mr. Bean, Clap Cat and more), rotating by default; make your own too.
- A small alarm you choose (jingle, buzz, bell, beep, chime, or off) and the character reads the line out loud.

## Install

1. Download the zip and open it.
2. Open **Stop Everything**. It offers to move itself to Applications and walks you through the setup (one minute).
3. First open on this build: macOS may ask you to confirm in **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway**.

## Privacy

Your calendar never leaves your Mac. No servers, no analytics. Details: https://stopeverything.pages.dev/privacy
