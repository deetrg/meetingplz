# MeetingPlz

Two minutes before a meeting, a meme takes over your Mac screen (or a plane flies by with your reminder) until you join.

**[Download for Mac](https://github.com/deetrg/meetingplz/releases/latest/download/MeetingPlz-mac-arm64.zip)** · Free · macOS 14+ · Apple silicon · https://meetingplz.pages.dev

## What it does

- Connect Google Calendar, or paste any calendar link.
- Two minutes before a meeting (30 s to 10 min, your choice): Meme takeover (9 reaction memes, rotating) or Fly-by banner (a plane crosses your screen, the reminder sits on top of your work).
- One button joins the call. Routines, tasks and solo calendar blocks are left alone.
- Pick an alarm (jingle, buzz, bell, beep, chime, off); the character can read the reminder out loud.

## Install

1. Download the zip and open it.
2. Open **MeetingPlz**. It moves itself to Applications and walks you through setup.
3. This build is not notarized yet: macOS may ask you to confirm in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway.

## Privacy

Your calendar never leaves your Mac. No servers, no analytics. https://meetingplz.pages.dev/privacy
