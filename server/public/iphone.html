MeetingPlz for iPhone

The iPhone app is in TestFlight. You can use the web app on your Home Screen right now.

Option 1: the app (TestFlight)

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. Open the invite: testflight.apple.com/join/eehmYguZ and tap Accept, then Install. (Public beta: if the page says the beta is not accepting testers yet, Apple is still reviewing it. Use option 2 meanwhile.)
  3. Open MeetingPlz, sign in with Google, and allow notifications.

Option 2: add the web app to your Home Screen

  1. Open meetingplz.nonolingo.com/app in Safari and sign in.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from the Home Screen and tap Turn on notifications.
